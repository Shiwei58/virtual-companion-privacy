Privacy Policy — XiaoYu Companion (小语伴侣)

Last updated: October 3, 2026

1. Overview

XiaoYu Companion is an AI chat companion application. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This app does not operate its own servers and does not collect or store your personal data on our side.

2. Data Processing

  • Chat messages: Text you send in conversations is transmitted to the third-party large language model API service that YOU configure in the app (e.g., Zhipu AI / DeepSeek / OpenAI-compatible endpoints), solely for generating replies. That provider processes the request under its own privacy policy.
  • Voice input: Your speech is converted to text on your device by the on-device speech recognition service (Core Speech Kit). Audio is not uploaded to us.
  • Voice output: Replies are synthesized to speech on your device.
  • Local storage: Your chat history, API key and preferences are stored only on your device (app preferences). Uninstalling the app deletes them.

3. Third-Party Services

  • The LLM API provider you configure (processes chat content to generate replies).
  • Huawei Core Speech Kit (on-device speech recognition and synthesis).

4. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Your Control

You can delete all chat history at any time from within the app, and remove all locally stored data by uninstalling the app.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: shiwei58-dev@example.com (replace with your contact before review if needed)

隐私政策 — 小语伴侣（XiaoYu Companion）

更新日期：2026年10月3日

一、总览

小语伴侣是一款 AI 聊天陪伴应用。本应用不运营自有服务器，我方不收集或存储你的任何个人数据。

二、数据处理方式

  • 聊天内容：你在对话中发送的文本会传输至你自己在应用内配置的第三方大模型 API 服务（如智谱 AI / DeepSeek 等 OpenAI 兼容接口），仅用于生成回复，该服务商按其自身隐私政策处理数据。
  • 语音输入：你的语音由设备端语音识别服务（Core Speech Kit）在本机转换为文字，不会上传至我方。
  • 语音播报：回复内容在设备端本地合成语音。
  • 本地存储：聊天记录、API 密钥与偏好设置仅保存在你的设备上，卸载应用即被删除。

三、第三方服务

  • 你自行配置的大模型 API 服务商（处理聊天内容以生成回复）。
  • 华为 Core Speech Kit（设备端语音识别与合成）。

四、未成年人保护

本应用不面向 13 周岁以下（欧洲经济区为 16 周岁以下）儿童，我们不会刻意收集儿童个人信息。

五、你的控制权

你可随时在应用内删除全部聊天记录；卸载应用即可删除所有本地数据。

六、联系方式

隐私问题请联系：shiwei58-dev@example.com
